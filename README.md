# Campaign Outlier Analysis

## Project Overview

This project focuses on identifying outliers and anomalies within marketing campaign performance data.

The objective was to detect unusual spending patterns, abnormal campaign behavior, and reporting inconsistencies that could impact business decisions.

---

## Business Problem

Campaign datasets often contain:

- Unusual spending behavior
- Unexpected sales variations
- Reporting anomalies
- Data inconsistencies
- Performance outliers

These anomalies can distort reporting and affect business decisions.
